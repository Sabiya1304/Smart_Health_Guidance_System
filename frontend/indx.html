Understand Your Health Better

This information helps you recognize symptoms, understand possible causes, and take better care of your health.

🧠 What are symptoms?

Symptoms are signals from your body that indicate something may not be functioning normally. They help you become aware of changes in your health condition.

  • Symptoms are physical or mental changes you feel in your body.
  • They can be mild, moderate, or severe depending on the condition.
  • Different diseases can show similar symptoms.
  • Early attention can help prevent complications.

⚠️ When should you be careful?

Some symptoms should not be ignored because they may indicate serious health issues.

  • High fever that lasts more than 2–3 days
  • Severe chest pain or breathing difficulty
  • Sudden dizziness or loss of balance
  • Continuous vomiting or dehydration
  • Severe headache that does not improve

If you experience severe or unusual symptoms, consult a healthcare professional immediately.

🌿 Healthy habits for better immunity

Good daily habits reduce the risk of many diseases and improve overall health.

  • Drink enough water throughout the day
  • Eat a balanced diet including fruits and vegetables
  • Sleep at least 7–8 hours daily
  • Do regular physical activity like walking or exercise
  • Maintain hygiene and wash hands frequently

📊 Common symptoms guide

Headache

Possible causes: stress, dehydration, infection

Care: rest, hydration, reduce screen time

Fever

Possible causes: viral or bacterial infection

Care: rest, fluids, temperature monitoring

Cough

Possible causes: cold, allergy, infection

Care: warm fluids, avoid dust and smoke

Fatigue

Possible causes: lack of sleep, stress, anemia

Care: proper sleep, nutrition improvement

💡 Healthy lifestyle tips

  • Maintain a balanced and nutritious diet
  • Stay physically active every day
  • Manage stress through relaxation techniques
  • Avoid smoking and unhealthy habits
  • Take care of your mental health

🏥 When to consult a doctor?

  • When symptoms last more than a few days
  • When symptoms become worse over time
  • When you experience severe pain or discomfort
  • When you notice unusual changes in your body

This system is designed for awareness only and does not replace professional medical advice.
